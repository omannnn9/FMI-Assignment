Detailed of Bullet Points

1. Digital transformation and cost reduction

What to say:
- Banks must invest in online banking, mobile apps, automation
- Reduces branch costs and operational expenses
- Improves customer convenience
Mauritius examples:
- MCB Juice App for payments and transfers
- SBM Internet Banking and digital onboarding
- Reduction of physical branch visits
Link to profit:
- Lower operating costs
- Higher transaction volumes
- Better customer retention

2. Improving risk management and credit control

What to say:
- Economic fallout increases loan defaults
- Banks must strengthen credit assessment and monitoring
- Focus on quality lending rather than risky growth
Mauritius examples:
- Banks restructuring loans for SMEs post-COVID 
- More stringent loan approval processes at MCB and SBM
Link to profit:
- Fewer bad debts
- Stable long-term income

3. Diversification of products and revenue streams

What to say:
- Relying only on traditional loans is risky
- Banks should offer insurance, investment products, wealth management
Mauritius examples:
- MCB Group offering investment funds and insurance
- SBM Life Insurance products
- Bancassurance services
Link to profit:
- Fee-based income
- Reduced dependence on interest income

4. Customer-centric approach and service quality

What to say:
- Customers compare banks more than ever
- Personalised services and better customer experience are essential
Mauritius examples:
- Dedicated relationship managers
- Loyalty programs and tailored SME solutions
- Faster complaint resolution via digital channels
Link to profit:
- Customer retention
- Cross-selling opportunities

5. Sustainability and long-term strategic repositioning

What to say:
- Banks must prepare for the future: ESG, green finance, financial inclusion
- Align with global and regional trends
Mauritius examples:
- Green loans for solar energy
- Financing sustainable projects
- Support for SMEs and start-ups
Link to profit:
- Long-term growth
- Better reputation and regulatory alignment
